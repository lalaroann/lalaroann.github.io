DoomScroll Studios' Mission:

“At the heart of our identity lies the concept of 'doomscrolling': an endless cycle of content that captures the fleeting attention span of the modern age.

We channel these challenges into boundless innovation and interactive experiences.”

The rebrand was inspired by the concept of "brainrotted Gen-Z creators" and the chaotic nature of infinite scrolling. I aimed to capture the essence of this digital culture while creating a visually striking and cohesive brand identity.

The logo features bold typography and dynamic shapes, reflecting the energy and creativity of the studio. The color palette combines vibrant hues with muted tones, creating a balance between chaos and sophistication. Overall, this project showcases my ability to create a compelling brand identity that resonates with a specific audience while maintaining a strong visual presence.

Typography:

Inter is DoomScroll Studios' chosen typeface for its high legibility on screens and its timeless, neutral design. Its geometric sans-serif structure ensures clarity across various platforms and devices.

With only one typeface family, the brand chose to vary its weight to differentiate types of communication from Extrabold, Bold, Medium, Regular, and Light.

Additionally, Inter's widespread availability makes it accessible for both internal and external use, maintaining consistency in branding without licensing barriers.

Why I Chose These Colors:

The brand takes up the word doomscrolling as a correlation of the brand being GenZ and hearing the term resonate a negative aspect of "over consuming media." As for the brand, I dont want to sit on too much on the negative side of it's definition but to make an impression of our own by referring to the term (DoomScroll). So I did my best to have that juxtaposition within the brand's identity and have it's own definition.

Trying to avoid negative connotations that it's trying to communicate such as “negative” colors: (Red = Danger , Yellow = Warning) and also not trying too hard to flip out the definition: (Green = Positive/Safe, Blue = Intellect).

Which is where I had the thought of using Cyan because even then it is more of a neutral color to use for technology, and a combination of Black for sophistication.

In this case, the Saturated Pink color works because this gives a strong sense of indicator of positive or negative when it comes to design, but only left with "what kind of design studio DoomScroll is?"
